ABOUT THIS SKY

Cassiopeia, Cygnus, and Lyra are drawn from real star positions.
Each figure uses a local gnomonic projection of J2000 coordinates, then a
uniform scale and translation to fit the page. The same positions are used
for the visible stars and their cursor-revealed connections.

These are separately arranged constellation studies, not a live sky map
or a simulation for a particular time and location. Background stars are
decorative. Orbital ellipses illustrate geometry and are not measured orbits.
